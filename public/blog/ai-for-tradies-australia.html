AI for Tradies: What Actually Works in 2026

You're brilliant at what you do. The plumbing, the sparky work, the building — that's sorted. It's the quoting, invoicing, scheduling, and chasing that surrounds the actual work that's eating your week alive. Here's where AI can genuinely help.

What's actually worth automating?

Let's skip the hype. AI for trade businesses in Australia isn't about robots replacing plumbers. It's about handling the admin that you're currently doing at 9pm on your couch when you should be watching telly. The three areas where AI makes the biggest difference for tradies are:

  • Quoting and estimates: AI can draft quotes from job photos, notes, and your pricing history. You review and send — what used to take 30 minutes takes 5.
  • Scheduling and route optimisation: Smart booking systems that factor in travel time, job duration, and customer preferences. Less windshield time, more billable hours.
  • Invoicing and follow-up: Automatic invoice generation when a job is marked complete. Automatic follow-up reminders. No more chasing payments at midnight.

The typical result: Australian tradies who automate these three areas save 5-10 hours per week on admin. That's an extra half-day to a full day back — either for more jobs or for having a life.

How much does AI automation cost for a trade business?

For a typical Australian trade business, expect to invest between $2,000 and $15,000 for initial setup, depending on how many systems you're connecting and how custom the solution needs to be. Ongoing costs for AI tools typically run $50-200 per month for scheduling, quoting, and invoicing automation combined.

Most tradies see return on investment within 2-3 months through time savings alone — before you even count the extra jobs you can fit in.

What doesn't need AI?

Here's the honest bit. Not everything needs AI. Some things that trade businesses think need AI actually just need:

  • A shared calendar (not AI — just a calendar everyone can see)
  • A proper CRM instead of a notebook (doesn't need to be smart, just needs to exist)
  • Templates for common emails and messages (copy-paste is fine)

A good AI consultant will tell you this upfront. If you can solve it with a $20/month tool and 20 minutes of setup, that's the right answer.

How to get started

  1. Write down what eats your time. For one week, note every admin task that takes more than 10 minutes. You'll spot the patterns fast.
  2. Pick the biggest time sink. Don't try to automate everything at once. Pick the one thing that would make the biggest difference if it just... happened automatically.
  3. Talk to someone who gets trade businesses. Generic AI consultants will try to sell you enterprise software. Find someone who understands that you need it to work from the ute between jobs.
